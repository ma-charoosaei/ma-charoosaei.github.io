I am a **Doctoral Researcher in Computer Science** at the [Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT)](https://www.uni.lu/snt-en/) at the University of Luxembourg.

My research focuses on **AI-enabled software testing**, including software testing agents, automated program repair, test evolution, and applications of large language models to software engineering.

I have research experience across software engineering, and applications of artificial intelligence. Before joining the University of Luxembourg, I worked as a Senior AI Research Assistant at Manchester Metropolitan University and as a Data Science Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Leeds.

I have published peer-reviewed research and completed more than 150 reviews for journals and conferences published by IEEE, ACM, Springer Nature, and Elsevier. I also received the **UK Global Talent endorsement** from Tech Nation.

## Selected Projects

### Automated Software Testing and Repair Agents

Research on agent-based software testing, automated program repair, test evolution, flaky test repair, and large language models for software engineering.

### Handwritten Logic Circuit Analysis

Developed computer-vision methods for analysing handwritten logic circuits. Compared YOLO, Faster R-CNN, RetinaNet, and Detectron2 and developed a boundary-tracking method for interpreting circuit connections.

### Small Metal Object Classification

Created and analysed datasets of small metal objects. Applied HOG and LBP feature extraction with k-nearest neighbours, support vector machines, and Naive Bayes, and compared these methods with deep-learning models.

### Vehicle Speed Estimation

Developed a system using YOLO object detection, vehicle tracking, and perspective-transformation techniques to estimate vehicle speeds from road video footage.

### Medical Image Classification and Segmentation

Worked on projects involving COVID-19 classification from chest X-rays, breast ultrasound image segmentation, brain tumour classification, MRI analysis, and melanoma classification.

### Pose Estimation

Implemented human pose-estimation pipelines using MediaPipe and OpenPose to detect and connect body keypoints across video frames.

### Face Detection and Facial Expression Analysis

Developed face-detection systems using YOLO and real-time facial-expression analysis using Haar cascades, VGG-Face, and DeepFace-based techniques.

### Telegram Data Dashboard

Developed a Streamlit dashboard for storing, searching, and analysing Telegram messages using a structured database.
