I am a **Doctoral Researcher in Computer Science** at the [Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT)](https://www.uni.lu/snt-en/), University of Luxembourg, supervised by [Prof. Mike Papadakis](https://www.uni.lu/snt-en/people/michail-papadakis/).

My research is on **AI-enabled software testing**: software testing agents, automated program repair, test evolution, and how large language models can be applied to software engineering problems. The through-line is using intelligent techniques to make software quality assurance more effective and less manual.

Before Luxembourg I was a Senior AI Research Assistant at Manchester Metropolitan University and a Data Science Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Leeds. I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Distinction) from Manchester Metropolitan University, and I have published peer-reviewed work in computer vision and machine learning.

I have also completed **more than 150 peer reviews** for journals and conferences published by IEEE, ACM, Springer Nature and Elsevier, and hold the **UK Global Talent endorsement** from Tech Nation.

## Research Interests

- Software testing and test evolution
- Automated program repair
- LLMs for software engineering
- Software testing agents
