---
title: "Small Metal Object Classification"
excerpt: "Classification of small metal objects using traditional machine learning and deep learning."
collection: portfolio
---

Created and analysed datasets of small metal objects.

Applied HOG and LBP feature extraction with k-nearest neighbours, support vector machines, and Naive Bayes, and compared the results with deep-learning models including YOLO, RetinaNet, Detectron2, Faster R-CNN, VGG16, VGG19, and AlexNet.
